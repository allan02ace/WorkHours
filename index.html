Import Data

Paste from your biometrics spreadsheet: select the day-number row and the times row for your name in Excel, copy, and paste below as-is (works even with multiple punches stacked in one cell). Set the month/year first since the sheet only shows day numbers. Or paste one line per punch: date, time-in, time-out. If a punch has no match, it's defaulted rather than skipped: a lone morning punch gets a time-out 8 hours later, and a lone afternoon/evening punch gets an 8:00 AM time-in.

Restore from a backup file

Upload a .xlsx, .xls, or .csv file — like a Timesheet backup this app exported earlier — and it'll restore full days along with their Location, Client, Work Accomplished and Approver details. Days that already exist will have their work items merged in rather than duplicated.
